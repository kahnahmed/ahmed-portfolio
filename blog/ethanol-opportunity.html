Balrampur vs Triveni vs EID Parry: My Thoughts on India's Ethanol Opportunity

June 2026 · 5 min read

Over the past few months, I've found myself returning to India's ethanol opportunity again and again.

What initially looked like a simple investment theme turned out to be much broader. The more I read, the more I realized that the story touches energy security, agriculture, government policy, and long-term economic priorities.

Three companies repeatedly appeared in my research: Balrampur Chini Mills, Triveni Engineering, and EID Parry.

At first glance, they seem similar. All three participate in the sugar and ethanol ecosystem. But the deeper I looked, the more I felt they represented three very different ways of thinking about the same opportunity.

These are my thoughts as I continue learning about the sector.

Why the Ethanol Theme Caught My Attention

What drew me to the ethanol theme wasn't just the possibility of stock returns.

I found it interesting because a single policy initiative has the potential to impact multiple parts of the economy at once.

What surprised me was that the ethanol story isn't really about sugar. At its core, it is about reducing fuel imports, supporting farmers, and creating additional sources of revenue for businesses that were historically dependent on sugar prices.

That makes the opportunity larger than it first appears.

One Theme, Three Different Businesses

One thing I initially got wrong was assuming that all sugar companies were largely the same.

From a distance, they seemed to be participating in the same ethanol opportunity. But the more I read, the more I realized that each company offered a different way to benefit from the theme.

That realization became the starting point for this article.

Balrampur Chini Mills: The Focused Ethanol Story

Of the three, Balrampur appears to be the most direct ethanol play. When investors discuss ethanol beneficiaries, Balrampur is usually among the first names mentioned.

My current impression is that Balrampur offers the clearest and most direct exposure to the ethanol theme.

Triveni Engineering: Ethanol Plus Diversification

Triveni feels different. While the company benefits from sugar and ethanol, it also has engineering businesses that contribute to the overall story.

Compared to Balrampur, Triveni feels less like a pure ethanol bet and more like a diversified industrial company with meaningful ethanol exposure.

EID Parry: The Diversified Approach

EID Parry was probably the most interesting company for me to study.

If Balrampur represents focus and Triveni represents balance, EID Parry represents diversification.

My Current Takeaway

  • Balrampur represents the strongest direct exposure to the ethanol opportunity.
  • Triveni represents a balance between ethanol exposure and business diversification.
  • EID Parry represents the most diversified business among the three.

Final Thoughts

If there's one thing I've taken away from studying this sector, it's that themes are often easier to understand than businesses.

The ethanol opportunity may sound straightforward, but the companies participating in it are very different.

At the moment, I see Balrampur as a focused ethanol story, Triveni as a diversified industrial story with ethanol exposure, and EID Parry as a broader agriculture and business group with ethanol exposure.

Whether these impressions prove correct over time remains to be seen. For now, this is simply where my research has led me.
